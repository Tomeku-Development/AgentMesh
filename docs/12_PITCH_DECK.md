# How to Use This Deck

Each section below is one slide. The **headline** is what's on screen; the **talk track** is what the presenter says (≈ 20–40 seconds per slide). Target length: 12 slides, ~6 minutes.

---

# Slide 1 — Title

**On screen:**
AgentMesh
*The Operating System for Autonomous Organizations.*
Built on Casper.

**Talk track:**
"Software today assists people. We're building software that *reasons, collaborates, and executes* — autonomous organizations made of AI agents, with every critical action verified on-chain."

---

# Slide 2 — The Problem

**On screen:**
Organizations are slow, expensive, and opaque.

* Human bottlenecks: meetings, approvals, email.
* Expensive specialists: legal, finance, compliance.
* Fragmented AI: assistants that can't collaborate.
* No verifiable trust.

**Talk track:**
"Real work needs many specialists coordinating. Today's AI is single-player. It can recommend, but it can't safely *act*, and it can't *prove* why it decided."

---

# Slide 3 — The Insight

**On screen:**
Organizations succeed because **specialists collaborate.**
A CEO doesn't do legal reviews. A lawyer doesn't manage treasury.

**Talk track:**
"We bring that same model to AI. Not one assistant — a *team* of specialized agents, each with memory, tools, permissions, and a wallet."

---

# Slide 4 — The Solution

**On screen:**
AgentMesh — a multi-agent mesh that analyzes, debates, reaches consensus, and executes on Casper.

**Talk track:**
"You submit a goal. Specialist agents analyze in parallel, debate, and reach consensus. Approved actions execute through Casper smart contracts — permanently auditable."

---

# Slide 5 — How It Works

**On screen (diagram):**

```text
Request → Coordinator → [Market · Finance · Legal · Risk] → Consensus → Treasury → Casper → Audit Trail
```

**Talk track:**
"A coordinator routes the task. Specialists contribute evidence. A consensus engine resolves it. The treasury agent executes on-chain. Every step is recorded."
