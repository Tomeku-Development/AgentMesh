# Brand Essence

AgentMesh is the operating system for autonomous organizations.

The brand should feel like the control room of an intelligent organization: **fast, precise, premium, and trustworthy.**

One word: **Confidence.**

---

# Positioning

**Category:** Autonomous AI organizations on-chain.

**Promise:** Delegate not just analysis, but trusted action.

**For:** Builders, enterprises, and decentralized communities who need AI that can collaborate and execute with verifiable trust.

**Tagline:** *The Operating System for Autonomous Organizations.*

# Logo

* **Primary mark:** the AgentMesh wordmark.
* **Variants:** White (on dark), Orange (accent), Black (on light).
* **Clear space:** minimum padding equal to the cap height of the wordmark on all sides.
* **Minimum size:** 24px height digital.

**Usage**

* Prefer the white wordmark on dark surfaces (default).
* Use the orange wordmark sparingly, for focal/brand moments.
* Use the black wordmark only on light backgrounds.

**Do not**

* Recolor, stretch, rotate, or add effects to the logo.
* Place the white wordmark on light or low-contrast backgrounds.

Files: `Images/AgentMesh-Logo/` (White / Orange / Black RGB).

# Typography

* **Display / Headings:** Space Grotesk (Sora acceptable as display alternative).
* **Body / UI:** Inter.
* **Mono:** JetBrains Mono (addresses, code, metrics).

Headlines may use uppercase italic with tight tracking for brand impact. Body stays sentence case, 1.5–1.75 line height.

# Voice & Tone

**Voice:** confident, precise, plain-spoken. We explain, we don't hype.

**Tone shifts by context:**

* Product UI — terse and clear.
* Marketing — bold and visionary, still grounded.
* Docs — neutral and exact.

**Principles**

* Show, don't tell. Prefer concrete claims over adjectives.
* Avoid hyperbole ("revolutionary", "best-ever").
* Short sentences. Active voice.

**Examples**

* ✅ "Agents collaborate, reach consensus, and execute on Casper."
* ❌ "The most revolutionary AI platform ever built."

# Imagery

* Dark, cinematic, high-contrast.
* Cyberpunk city / network motifs, used with restraint.
* Orange as the accent light source.
* Glass, blur, soft shadows, neon edges.
* Avoid stock-photo clichés and literal robots.

---

# Motion

* Mechanical, fast, purposeful — never playful.
* Standard transition 200ms; entrances use ease-out-expo.
* Animate transform/opacity only.
* Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Tools: GSAP, Framer Motion, Lenis.

---

# Do & Don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Keep dark-first contrast high | Use light gray text on dark |
| Use orange for focus/action | Flood large areas with orange |
| Use SVG icons (Lucide) | Use emojis as icons |
| Keep the logo unmodified | Recolor or distort the logo |
| Write plainly and confidently | Over-hype or over-explain |
