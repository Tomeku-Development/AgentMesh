# Landscape Overview

The agent space splits into three groups:

1. **Agent frameworks** — developer libraries to build agents (CrewAI, LangGraph, AutoGen).
2. **On-chain / Web3 agents** — agents tied to crypto ecosystems (ElizaOS, Virtuals).
3. **Enterprise automation** — RPA and workflow tools adding AI (UiPath, Zapier, n8n).

AgentMesh sits at a distinct intersection: a **platform** for multi-agent organizations with **verifiable on-chain execution**.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

* **Multi-agent collaboration** — can specialized agents coordinate?
* **Action execution** — can agents safely *act*, not just recommend?
* **On-chain trust** — are decisions verifiable and auditable?
* **Organization model** — roles, permissions, wallets, governance?
* **Marketplace / extensibility** — can third parties add agents?
* **Enterprise readiness** — security, oversight, compliance.

# Framework Players

**CrewAI, LangGraph, AutoGen** are excellent *building blocks*. They help developers compose agents and workflows.

* **Strength:** flexibility, community, rapid prototyping.
* **Gap:** no native verifiable execution; no organization/governance model; trust is implicit.

**AgentMesh relationship:** complementary. AgentMesh *uses* LangGraph internally and adds the organization, consensus, and on-chain trust layers on top.

---

# On-Chain / Web3 Agent Players

**ElizaOS, Virtuals** bring agents into crypto ecosystems, often around tokens and social presence.

* **Strength:** on-chain identity, token incentives, community traction.
* **Gap:** focused on individual agents/characters rather than coordinated organizations performing verifiable enterprise workflows.

**AgentMesh relationship:** differentiated. We focus on *organizations of agents* doing real work (due diligence, treasury, compliance) with auditable decisions on Casper.

---

# Enterprise Automation

**UiPath, Zapier, n8n** automate business processes and are adding AI.

* **Strength:** distribution, integrations, enterprise trust.
* **Gap:** deterministic automation, not reasoning organizations; no verifiable on-chain trust; limited true multi-agent collaboration.

**AgentMesh relationship:** category-adjacent. We target reasoning-heavy, trust-sensitive workflows they cannot serve.

# Where AgentMesh Wins

1. **Organization model** — roles, memory, permissions, wallets, governance — not just agents.
2. **Verifiable execution** — decisions recorded immutably on Casper.
3. **Consensus engine** — explainable multi-agent decision-making.
4. **Trust-sensitive use cases** — finance, compliance, governance, RWA.
5. **Casper-native rails** — MCP and x402 for the agent economy.

The defensible wedge: **trust**. Frameworks give you agents; AgentMesh gives you *verifiable autonomous organizations*.
