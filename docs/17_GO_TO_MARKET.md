# GTM Thesis

AgentMesh is **developer-first**, then **community-led**, then **enterprise-expanded**.

Adoption starts with builders who integrate agents and ship workflows. Their work seeds a marketplace and community, which becomes the credibility and pipeline for enterprise pilots.

```text
Developers → Community → Enterprise
(adopt)      (amplify)    (monetize)
```

# Target Segments

| Segment | Why they care | Entry workflow |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Web3 builders / DAOs | Verifiable governance & treasury | DAO governance, treasury automation |
| AI engineers / startups | Multi-agent orchestration that executes | Research, due diligence |
| Funds / VC | Autonomous due diligence with audit trail | Investment committee |
| Enterprises | Compliance & RWA with verifiable trust | Compliance automation, RWA verification |

Beachhead: **Web3-native funds and DAOs** — they already value on-chain verifiability and feel coordination pain acutely.

# Funnel

```text
Awareness   → demos, hackathons, content, social
Interest    → docs, SDK, free tier, sandbox
Activation  → first org created + first on-chain execution
Retention   → recurring workflows, marketplace usage
Revenue     → paid tiers, marketplace share, enterprise
Referral    → build-in-public, agent publishers
```

**Activation moment:** a builder runs their first multi-agent workflow that executes on Casper.

# Pricing & Packaging

| Tier | Audience | Includes |

| **Pro** | Startups/teams | Higher limits, custom orgs, workflow builder |

Future: agent-economy **protocol fees** (see `19_TOKENOMICS.md`). Usage-based AI costs are passed through transparently.
